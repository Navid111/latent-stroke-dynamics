# 2026-08-23 — Latent-space planner

The post-formal planner protocol, six latent hashes, smoke, and controlled comparison were completed in one guarded workflow. Foundation validation and all runner tests passed before data. The smoke completed with integrity and indicated latent MSE might outperform latent ranking.

The separately authorized controlled comparison then evaluated six untouched targets, five methods, 100 steps, and 128 candidates. All targets completed and every integrity check passed. Mean final MSE was 0.146905 random, 0.045569 exact pixel, 0.053630 learned pixel, 0.076142 latent MSE, and 0.090934 latent ranking.

Latent ranking improved every target and beat random by 38.10%, but it was 1.996× exact-pixel error and therefore failed the frozen maximum ratio of 1.5×. It was also 1.194× latent-MSE error. Top-1/top-5 exact candidate rates were 5%/13% for ranking and 9.5%/27.2% for latent MSE. Mean score-to-exact Spearman was 0.426 versus 0.549.

This preserves the formal success while establishing its boundary: ranking improved four-way one-step retrieval but did not produce the stronger 128-candidate long-horizon planner. The controlled run is closed permanently. No rerun, retuning, or training on controlled targets is permitted.
