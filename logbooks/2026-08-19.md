### Matching validation

For every visible pair, `sparse_pixel_matched_noise` and `add_stroke` matched both changed-pixel count and pixel MAE exactly or to stored integer precision. The sparse pixels were distributed outside the coherent reference-stroke region.

### Gate diagnostics

| Crowding | Add > sparse top-10% | Add > sparse reference region | Median localization lift | Median reference enrichment | No-change max |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 100% | 100% | 12.40× | 2.05× | 2.98e-7 |
| 5 | 0% | 100% | 11.52× | 5.54× | 3.58e-7 |

### Interpretation

The representation preserved the coherent stroke in the correct spatial region under both blank and moderate crowding. Sparse random pixels generated larger fixed top-10% responses under crowding because the same pixel budget was dispersed across far more patch locations. This is a robustness finding, not evidence that the coherent line disappeared: reference-region separation, localization lift, enrichment, and heatmaps all remained strong.

The formal gate therefore uses paired reference-region separation and localization as primary metrics. Fixed top-10%, dense noise, and global-token behavior remain mandatory secondary diagnostics.
