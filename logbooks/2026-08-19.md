### Initial observations

- Identical pairs produced numerical-zero distances, validating the basic distance path.
- On blank canvases, `add_stroke` produced strong and consistent changes:
  - global cosine distance: approximately `0.381 ± 0.010`,
  - mean patch cosine distance: approximately `0.352 ± 0.013`,
  - maximum patch distance: approximately `0.788 ± 0.057`.
- The example heatmap's strongest response followed the added line, providing encouraging qualitative localization evidence.
- With five prior strokes, mean add-stroke patch distance (`~0.093`) was close to tiny-noise distance (`~0.088`) and highly variable.
- Moving a stroke changed the global token little while producing stronger local maxima, supporting spatial features over a global-only representation.
- Intensity-only changes were weak compared with adding a stroke or changing width.
- Tiny distributed noise caused unexpectedly large feature changes on a blank canvas.

### Decision

The smoke test validated the implementation and gave encouraging blank-canvas evidence, but it did **not** pass Gate 1. Three samples are insufficient, and the first design confounded crowding with different random actions while relying too heavily on all-patch means.
