# 2026-08-21 — Final paired-control result

## Latent Gate 2

The frozen latent run formally failed. It achieved strong average-error reduction but only 27.7% four-way retrieval, with width pairwise accuracy of 40.7%.

## Pixel protocol, implementation, and smoke

The explanatory pixel protocol was committed before implementation. All 24 tests passed. The development-only smoke achieved 93.75% retrieval and passed every oracle and integrity check, authorizing the unchanged paired run.

## Paired pixel-space control

The single frozen paired run completed with `paired_control_eligible: true` and `control_status: success`. Validation selected the 833-parameter MLP.

- identity action-region MSE: 0.499396;
- mean-delta action-region MSE: 0.475834;
- selected MLP action-region MSE: 0.000249;
- improvement versus identity: 99.950%;
- improvement versus mean delta: 99.948%;
- retrieval: 100% across all three seeds and 300 examples;
- pairwise position, width, and intensity win rates: 100%;
- crowding improvements: 99.973%, 99.955%, and 99.907%;
- exact-oracle retrieval: 100%;
- exact-oracle maximum action-region MSE: `3.55e-15`;
- implementation sanity: passed.

The linear family also achieved 96.1% retrieval, although its pixel reconstruction error was far higher than the MLP’s.

## Interpretation

Exact action information is fully recoverable in the tested full-resolution pixel formulation. The latent failure is therefore not due to ambiguous actions, broken retrieval, insufficient data, or a general inability of a tiny deterministic model to learn the transition. It is localized to the overall latent patch formulation.

This does not prove DINOv2 alone is the problem because the pixel control changes target space, spatial resolution, and action-mask resolution together. The latent Gate 2 result remains a formal fail. The core experimental story is complete; plot review and thesis writing are next.
