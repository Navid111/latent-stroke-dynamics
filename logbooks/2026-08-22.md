# 2026-08-22 — Stage 3 controlled success and qualitative deployment limits

## Controlled success

The single authorized six-target run passed all frozen criteria. Learned mean final MSE was `0.060032`, versus `0.155971` random and `0.050479` exact. Learned planning improved every target, reduced final MSE by 61.51% versus random, and finished 18.93% above exact.

## Painter packaging and polarity repair

The root `paint.py` command was added and initially passed 36 tests. A white-on-black MNIST smoke exposed a renderer/target polarity mismatch: pixel MSE rewarded painting the dominant black background. Deterministic auto inversion repaired the target to dark-on-white. The expanded suite passed 38 tests.

The repaired 20-step learned smoke improved MSE by 64.22%, achieved 85% exact top-1 and 100% top-5 agreement among 32 candidates, and produced a recognizable angular 3.

## Full learned MNIST run

At 100 strokes and 128 candidates, learned final MSE was `0.040860`, a 71.70% improvement. Only 42 actions improved true MSE. Top-1/top-5 exact agreement fell to 23%/37%, mean exact rank was 9.23, and mean regret was `0.000410`. The curve reached its best state at step 33 and then deteriorated.

## Exact MNIST diagnostic

Exact greedy reached final MSE `0.022196`, an 84.62% improvement, with 88 improving actions. Its curve continued improving until step 99 and its painting was visibly cleaner and more recognizable. Learned final MSE was approximately 1.841 times exact.

The exact result shows that improving proposals remained available after the learned trajectory turned upward. The dominant late-stage issue is therefore learned ranking under crowded, out-of-distribution trajectories, although a no-op or stopping rule remains a sensible secondary improvement.

## Best-painting artifact

The user-facing command now preserves the lowest-MSE trajectory frame as `best_painting.png` while retaining the literal requested-budget final canvas. Best-step/error and final-degradation metrics are saved, and the comparison figure displays both. The output change does not modify trajectories or any controlled result. Local revalidation of the existing 38-test suite is next.
