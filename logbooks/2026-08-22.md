## Smoke implementation

Added a development-only random-versus-exact engineering smoke. It generates one fixed 20-stroke synthetic target and runs both methods for 20 selected strokes with 32 candidates per step.

The script saves target/final images, fixed-scale error panels, MSE progress curves, per-step CSV files, selected strokes, and animated GIFs. It replays the exact trajectory and fails if deterministic reproduction or finite-metric checks fail.

The smoke has no formal threshold and cannot revise earlier decisions. Its purpose is to validate behavior and artifact generation before adding a separately saved demonstration MLP checkpoint.
