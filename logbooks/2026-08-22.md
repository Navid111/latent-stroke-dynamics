# 2026-08-22 — Stage 3 success, qualitative limits, and extension freeze

## Completed core

The six-target controlled Stage 3 run passed all frozen criteria. Learned planning reduced mean final MSE by 61.51% versus random and finished 18.93% above exact greedy. The user-facing painter, polarity repair, and best-painting output were subsequently validated by 38 passing tests.

## Qualitative deployment result

On normalized MNIST-3 at 100 strokes, exact greedy reached final MSE `0.022196` with 88 improving actions and best step 99. Learned pixel planning reached `0.040860` with 42 improving actions and best step 33. The exact result localizes the dominant late-stage limitation to learned ranking in crowded out-of-distribution trajectories.

## Representation-extension freeze

Before implementing or generating any new extension data, froze a post-core explanatory comparison containing exactly two new representations:

1. deterministic unmasked frozen `facebook/vit-mae-base` final spatial tokens;
2. a small reconstruction-trained convolutional autoencoder producing a 32×16×16 latent.

The extension uses new untouched primary seeds `20261024`–`20261026`, four stress seeds `20261027`–`20261030`, and separate development seeds `20261020`–`20261022`. Historical DINOv2 and pixel outcomes remain descriptive anchors and are not rerun.

Each new representation uses the existing action encoding, balanced residual objective, linear/MLP baselines, three dynamics seeds, and four-way position/width/intensity retrieval. The 50% action-usable threshold and all integrity requirements were committed before implementation.

No extension data have been generated. Next work is implementation and unit testing only.
