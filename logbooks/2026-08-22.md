# 2026-08-22 — Stage 3 controlled success and first qualitative failure

## Single controlled run

All six target runs completed without failure. Eligibility and implementation integrity passed. Learned final MSE averaged `0.060032`, versus `0.155971` for random and `0.050479` for exact greedy. Learned planning improved every target, reduced mean final MSE by 61.51% versus random, and finished 18.93% above exact. All frozen criteria passed.

The learned planner achieved 33.5% exact top-1 and 58.67% exact top-5 agreement among 128 candidates. Mean exact rank was 7.51 and mean regret was `0.0003867`. Target 5 finished better under learned than exact greedy because exact is a local one-step oracle rather than a globally optimal sequence planner.

## Painter packaging and validation

Added the root `paint.py` command, reusable package module, complete artifact output, frozen-checkpoint validation, atomic publishing, and overwrite guards. The M1 repository suite passed all 36 tests in 2.81 seconds.

## First arbitrary-image smoke

The first learned smoke used a web image of an MNIST-style number 3 with a black background and white foreground. After 20 strokes and 32 candidates per step, final MSE decreased from `0.798784` to `0.533353`, but the output was visibly scattered and did not resemble the digit. Exact top-1 agreement was 15%, mean exact rank was 11.75, and mean regret was `0.011429`.

The monotonic 33.23% MSE improvement was misleading: most target pixels belonged to the black background, so dark strokes anywhere across the white canvas lowered the loss. The renderer could not produce the target polarity because it begins white and only adds dark strokes. The poor result is therefore a real domain/preprocessing failure rather than evidence of a successful digit painting.

## Polarity repair

Added deterministic qualitative-only polarity normalization. Auto mode measures the outer-border median after grayscale crop/resize and inverts when the border is dark. Explicit preserve/invert modes remain available. The command now saves both pre-polarity and normalized targets and records the decision in summary/config metadata.

Two parameterized tests cover light-on-dark inversion and dark-on-light preservation. The frozen checkpoint, controlled target generation, planner, and controlled result are unchanged. The failed smoke remains preserved as a limitation case. Local validation of the repaired suite is next.
