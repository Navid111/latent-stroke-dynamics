# 2026-08-22 — Stage 3 controlled success

## All-method smoke

The saved 833-parameter MLP successfully drove the sequential painter. After 20 actions on the development target, learned final MSE was `0.089196`, compared with `0.144913` for random and `0.074427` for exact greedy. Learned planning reduced error by 40.32%, improved 19 of 20 steps, and remained 19.84% above exact.

Exact top-1 agreement was 55% and top-5 agreement was 80%. Mean exact rank was 4.6 of 32 and mean regret was `0.000653`. One rank-25 error at step 7 briefly worsened the canvas, but replanning recovered. Deterministic replay passed. Visual results and fixed-scale error panels agreed with the metrics.

## Controlled freeze and validation

The six-target comparison was frozen before controlled data generation. It used 100 actions, 128 candidates per step, six fixed target/planner seed pairs, the validated local checkpoint, and committed success criteria. Validation passed with the expected checkpoint digest and generated no data.

## Single controlled run

All six target runs completed without failure. Eligibility and implementation integrity passed. Learned final MSE averaged `0.060032`, versus `0.155971` for random and `0.050479` for exact greedy. Learned planning improved every target, reduced mean final MSE by 61.51% versus random, and finished 18.93% above exact. All frozen criteria therefore passed.

The learned planner achieved 33.5% exact top-1 and 58.67% exact top-5 agreement among 128 candidates. Mean exact rank was 7.51 and mean regret was only `0.0003867`, explaining why non-top-1 choices still supported useful painting trajectories. Target 5 finished better under learned than exact greedy; exact is a local one-step oracle rather than a globally optimal sequence planner.

## Visual review

Random selection produced heavily cluttered canvases. Exact greedy generally reconstructed target geometry most cleanly. Learned paintings retained the dominant structures on every target and were visually much closer to exact than random. Thin lines and crowded intersections remained the clearest weaknesses. Aggregate learned error dropped rapidly and then plateaued around 60–70 strokes, suggesting that early stopping or an explicit no-op could improve a future version without affecting the frozen result.

## Decision and next action

The controlled result is frozen as a success and must not be rerun or retuned. Next, package the learned planner as an arbitrary-image command, run qualitative user-image demonstrations, and integrate the result into the thesis.
